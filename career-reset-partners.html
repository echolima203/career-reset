The philosophy behind the algorithm

Three layers, working together.

Career Reset isn't a content library — it's an architecture. A psychological storyline, evidence-based discovery tools, and a deeper logic of inner alignment.

Layer 1 · Journey

Psychological storyline

A change curve inspired by the Kübler-Ross model — the emotional arc of any major transition, mapped into five sequential levels.

  • Shock & denial → integration
  • Each level has a clear intention
  • Progress, never shame or streaks
Layer 2 · Discovery

Self-discovery tools

Built-in assessments and homework that turn reflection into evidence — and feed richer coaching conversations.

  • Ikigai — purpose & direction
  • Human Design — energy & decisions
  • Key motivating drivers · work-style profile
Layer 3 · Architecture

Philosophical logic

The inner structure of the exercises — drawn from archetypal models of inner development; the logic of alignment from confusion to conscious action.

  • Essence vs. conditioned self
  • Balance & transformation
  • Archetypal energies
Why a structured journey works.

Most apps hand people a pile of exercises and hope something sticks. Career Reset sequences everything along the emotional arc of change — so each person gets the right practice for where they actually are, in the right order, instead of a generic to-do list.

The algorithm · 5 levels

A guided emotional journey.
